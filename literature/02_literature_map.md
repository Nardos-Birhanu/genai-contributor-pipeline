# Literature Review Map

## Research Area

Generative AI and the sustainability of online knowledge communities:
the effect of AI on newcomer-to-contributor pathways.


## Core Research Question

Does generative AI disrupt the process through which newcomers become contributors in online knowledge communities?


# Literature Streams


## Stream 1: Foundational Theory
### Purpose
Explain why newcomers become contributors.

### Main Questions
- How do newcomers learn within communities?
- What role do early low-level contributions play?
- How do peripheral activities develop expertise?

### Expected Contribution
Provides the theoretical mechanism behind the newcomer-to-contributor pipeline.


## Stream 2: Empirical Studies of Contributor Emergence

### Purpose
Understand how previous studies measure newcomer progression.

### Main Questions
- How is newcomer conversion measured?
- What predicts becoming a contributor?
- How are participation trajectories modeled?


## Stream 3: Generative AI and Knowledge Production

### Purpose
Establish what is already known about AI changing contribution patterns.

### Main Questions
- Does AI replace human contribution?
- Does AI change participation incentives?
- Has AI been studied as affecting newcomer development?


## Stream 4: Stack Overflow and Online Q&A Communities

### Purpose
Provide context for the empirical setting.

### Main Questions
- How does Stack Overflow participation work?
- How are contributors created?
- What previous platform changes affected participation?


## Stream 5: Methodological Literature

### Purpose
Support research design decisions.

### Main Questions
- How are longitudinal transitions measured?
- How are cohort effects studied?
- What are challenges with survival analysis and causal inference?
